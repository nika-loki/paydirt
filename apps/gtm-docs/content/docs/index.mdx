Welcome to **paydirt** — an open-source library of governed go-to-market (GTM)
systems, and the home of **gtm-docs**, the documentation app you're reading.

Start where it fits your role:

- **[Systems](/docs/systems)** — the design records: every GTM workflow with its
  diagram, data model, attribution touch, and governance. Written for operators,
  RevOps, and anyone who needs to know how the machine works without reading code.
- **[Concepts](/docs/concepts)** — the shared vocabulary: trust model,
  environments, blast radius, dry-runs and budgets.
- **[Build & run](/docs/build)** — for GTM engineers: connector contracts,
  workflow templates, deployment recipes.
- **[Studio](/studio)** — author boards in the canvas: flow maps of your
  pipelines and ER models of your objects (paste JSON to infer fields), with
  import/export of the underlying documents and a copy-as-MDX export for
  design records. The [boards & studio guide](/docs/build/boards-and-studio)
  walks the whole authoring path.
- **[Glossary](/docs/glossary)** — single-word definitions of every term
  these pages use, cross-linked to each other.
- **[Reference](/docs/reference)** — pointers into the repo: the design spec
  section by section, security reporting rules, and the changelog.
